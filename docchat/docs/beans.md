# Bean Storage

Great espresso is impossible with poorly stored beans. Coffee is perishable: it
goes stale as it loses aromatics and reacts with oxygen, light, heat, and
moisture. Our storage rules exist to protect the recipe in the **Espresso Guide**.

## The four enemies

Keep beans away from **oxygen, light, heat, and moisture**. In practice:

- **Airtight containers only.** Transfer beans out of any opened bag into a
  sealed, opaque canister. Squeeze the air out and seal after every scoop.
- **Room temperature, 18–22 °C.** Store on a shelf away from the espresso machine
  and any window. Do **not** refrigerate or freeze working stock — condensation
  ruins it and the fridge imparts off-flavours.
- **Out of direct light.** Opaque containers in a cupboard are ideal.

## Freshness window

- **Rest:** Beans need about **3 days off roast** before they pull well; fresh
  roast is full of CO₂ and pulls gassy and uneven.
- **Peak:** Best flavour is roughly days 4 through 14 after roasting.
- **Use by:** Use beans within **21 days of the roast date** printed on the bag.
  After that, aromatics fade and no grind adjustment will fully recover them.

Rotate stock first-in, first-out. Always check the roast date before refilling the
Mazzer Major hopper.

## Don't overfill the hopper

Only put a few hours of beans in the grinder hopper at a time. Beans sitting in an
open hopper under the café lights go stale fast. At close, grind out or return the
hopper contents to their sealed canister, as noted in the **Closing Procedures**.
