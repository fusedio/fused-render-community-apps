# Opening Procedures

The opening barista sets the tone for the whole day. Arrive at **6:30 AM**; doors
open to customers at **7:00 AM**. That half hour is not slack — most of it is
equipment warm-up.

## Step by step

1. **6:30 — Power on the espresso machine.** Switch on the La Marzocco Linea PB
   first thing. It needs a full **30 minutes** to reach a stable brew temperature
   of 93 °C and group-head pressure. Everything else happens while it heats.
2. **6:35 — Start the batch brewer.** Fill and switch on the Fetco. Run one empty
   cycle of hot water through it to rinse out any overnight staleness.
3. **6:40 — Grinder and beans.** Fill the Mazzer Major hopper with today's
   espresso beans. Only pour in what you expect to use in the next few hours;
   see **Bean Storage** for why we never overfill the hopper.
4. **6:45 — Dial in.** Pull a calibration shot and adjust the grind until you hit
   the house recipe. The full dial-in method lives in the **Espresso Guide**.
5. **6:55 — Set the till.** Count the opening float of **$150** and confirm it
   against the log. Details are in **Cash Handling**.
6. **7:00 — Open the doors.**

## Notes

If the machine was left off overnight (check the closing log), give it the full
30 minutes before serving — a cold group head pulls sour, under-extracted shots
no matter how good your grind is. Never rush the warm-up to open early.
