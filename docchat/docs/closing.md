# Closing Procedures

Closing well means the opening barista walks into a clean, ready café. Last
orders are at **7:45 PM**; we lock the doors at **8:00 PM**. Do not start
breaking down the espresso station before last orders — a customer may still
want a final cup.

## Step by step

1. **7:45 — Last orders.** Stop taking new espresso orders. Begin wiping down the
   front counter and seating area.
2. **8:00 — Lock the doors.** Flip the sign and lock up.
3. **8:05 — Backflush the machine.** Backflush each group head of the La Marzocco
   with Cafiza, following the daily routine in the **Cleaning Schedule**. This is
   the single most important closing task for machine longevity.
4. **8:15 — Purge and empty the grinder.** Grind out the last of the hopper so no
   beans sit overnight in the Mazzer. Return unused beans to their airtight
   container per **Bean Storage**.
5. **8:20 — Count the till.** Reconcile the drawer, set aside the **$150** float
   for tomorrow, and complete the nightly deposit drop. Full instructions are in
   **Cash Handling**; any variance over $5 must be noted in the log.
6. **8:35 — Final wipe-down and bins.** Wipe all surfaces, take out the rubbish
   and recycling, and mop the floor behind the bar.
7. **8:45 — Set the machine.** Leave the La Marzocco **on** unless the closing log
   says otherwise, so the opener does not have to wait the full 30-minute warm-up.

## Handover

Write the closing note on the pass: anything low on stock, any equipment acting
up, and whether the machine was left on or off. The opener relies on this.
