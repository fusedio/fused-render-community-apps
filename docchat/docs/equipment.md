# Equipment

Meridian runs a small, well-maintained kit. Knowing each machine helps you spot
problems early and clean it correctly (see the **Cleaning Schedule**).

## La Marzocco Linea PB (espresso machine)

Our two-group espresso machine. It holds the brew water at **93 °C** and stable
group pressure once warmed. Key facts every barista needs:

- It takes a full **30 minutes** to warm up from cold — the first task in
  **Opening Procedures**.
- It is backflushed with Cafiza every night as part of closing.
- Purge each group head briefly before locking in the portafilter to clear any
  cooled water.

## Mazzer Major (espresso grinder)

Our flat-burr espresso grinder, dialled to roughly **3.2** as a starting
reference. The grind adjustment is how we hit the house recipe in the **Espresso
Guide**. Do not overfill the hopper — only a few hours of beans at a time, per
**Bean Storage**. The burrs are brushed clean weekly.

## Fetco (batch brewer)

Our filter-coffee brewer for high-volume service. Rinse it with a hot-water cycle
at open, and deep-clean the brew basket and spray head weekly.

## Reporting faults

If any machine behaves oddly — inconsistent temperature, grinder noise, leaks —
note it in the closing handover immediately and, for anything affecting safety,
tell the manager the same day. Never attempt an internal repair on the La
Marzocco yourself; it is plumbed and heated to high pressure.
