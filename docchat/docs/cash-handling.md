# Cash Handling

Handling money accurately protects you and the café. Count carefully, log
everything, and when in doubt, ask a manager.

## The float

The drawer starts and ends every day with a **$150 float** in mixed small
denominations. The opening barista counts it in during **Opening Procedures**;
the closing barista counts it back out during **Closing Procedures**.

## During service

- Count change back to the customer out loud.
- Keep large notes under the till tray, not in the open drawer.
- Do a quick mid-shift skim if the drawer gets heavy, and log the amount removed.

## Refunds

- Refunds of **$20 or less** can be processed by any barista; note the reason on
  the receipt.
- Refunds **over $20 require manager approval** before you process them. No
  exceptions, even for a regular.

## Closing the till

At close, count the drawer twice. Set aside the **$150** float for the next day
and prepare everything above that as the **nightly deposit drop**. Record the
day's total and note any variance:

- A variance of **$5 or less** is logged and left.
- A variance **over $5** must be flagged in the closing note and reported to the
  manager the next morning.

The full end-of-day sequence, including where the till count falls in the closing
order, is in the **Closing Procedures**.
