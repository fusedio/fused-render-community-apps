# New Barista Onboarding

Welcome to Meridian Coffee Roasters. This guide is the starting point for your
first two weeks behind the bar. Read it end to end, then keep it handy while you
shadow a senior barista.

## Your first shift

Arrive fifteen minutes before your rostered start so you have time to change,
wash your hands, and read the shift notes on the pass. Your trainer will walk you
through the floor, the till, and the espresso station.

For anything time-of-day specific, follow the dedicated guides:

- Morning shifts start with the **Opening Procedures**, including switching on the
  La Marzocco Linea PB, which needs a full 30 minutes to come up to pressure.
- Evening shifts end with the **Closing Procedures**, which cover backflushing,
  counting the till, and the nightly deposit drop.

## What you will learn to do first

1. **Pull a clean espresso.** Our house recipe is 18 g in, 36 g out, in 25–30
   seconds. The full method is in the **Espresso Guide**.
2. **Keep the station clean.** Backflushing and wipe-downs happen throughout the
   day, not just at close — see the **Cleaning Schedule**.
3. **Handle the till.** Floats, refunds, and drops are covered in **Cash
   Handling**. Refunds over $20 always need a manager.

## Culture

We taste before we serve. If a shot looks or smells off, dump it and pull
another — wasting 18 g of coffee is always cheaper than serving a bad cup. Ask
questions early and often. Nobody expects you to have the espresso dialled in on
day one; that skill comes from the daily calibration described in the Espresso
Guide.
