# Cleaning Schedule

A clean machine makes clean-tasting coffee and lasts for years. Cleaning is split
into daily, weekly, and monthly routines. The equipment referenced here is
described in **Equipment**.

## Daily (every closing shift)

- **Backflush the group heads with Cafiza.** Insert the blind (no-hole) basket,
  add a small measure of Cafiza detergent, and run the backflush cycle on each
  group head of the La Marzocco. Then backflush again with plain water to rinse.
  This is a mandatory step in the **Closing Procedures**.
- **Purge and wipe the steam wands** after every drink through the day, and give
  them a final wipe at close.
- **Wipe the grinder chute and hopper** and empty any beans left in the Mazzer.
- **Empty and rinse the drip tray.**

## Weekly

- **Soak the portafilters and baskets** in a warm Cafiza solution for 20–30
  minutes to strip built-up coffee oils, then rinse thoroughly.
- **Brush the grinder burrs.** Remove the Mazzer hopper and upper burr carrier and
  brush out old grounds; stale grounds in the burrs taste rancid.
- **Deep-clean the batch brewer** and its spray head.

## Monthly

- **Descale the machine** if you are on a hard-water supply. The La Marzocco is
  plumbed through a softener, so descaling is only needed if water tests hard.
- **Replace or check the water filter.**

Log every weekly and monthly task on the maintenance sheet so the next barista
knows what has been done.
