# Espresso Guide

Everything we serve starts from a well-pulled shot. Our house recipe is
deliberately simple so it is repeatable across every barista and every shift.

## The house recipe

- **Dose:** 18 g of ground coffee in the double basket.
- **Yield:** 36 g of liquid espresso in the cup — a **1:2 ratio**.
- **Time:** 25–30 seconds from the moment you start the pump.
- **Water temperature:** 93 °C (the La Marzocco Linea PB holds this once warmed).

If all four numbers land in range and it tastes balanced, you are done.

## Dialling in

The grind is the only variable you adjust day to day, because fresh beans change
as they rest and age (see **Bean Storage**). Start each morning with a
calibration shot:

- **Shot too fast (under 25 s), tastes sour and thin:** the grind is too coarse.
  Make it **finer** on the Mazzer Major — nudge toward a lower number.
- **Shot too slow (over 30 s), tastes bitter and harsh:** the grind is too fine.
  Make it **coarser** — nudge toward a higher number.

Our starting reference is roughly **3.2 on the Mazzer Major**, but treat that as a
hint, not a target — always dial to the 18 g / 36 g / 25–30 s window, not to a
number on the dial.

## Milk

Steam to a glossy microfoam, no large bubbles, to about 60–65 °C — hot enough to
be comfortable to drink but never scalded. Purge the steam wand before and after
every use; this is part of the ongoing hygiene in the **Cleaning Schedule**.

## When in doubt, dump it

A sour or gushing shot is not worth serving. Dump it, adjust the grind one step,
and pull again. The machine and grinder are described in **Equipment**.
